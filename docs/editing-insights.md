# Adding Insights & News Articles on the New Birdsey Group Website

This guide walks you through publishing a new article, start to finish. It is the companion to the team bios guide, and it assumes no technical background; everything happens in the same editing screen in your web browser.

## 1. What You'll Need

- **The editor address:** https://birdsey-group.vercel.app/admin
- **Your login.** The same editor account you use for team bios.
- **The article text**, ideally in a Word or Google doc so you can paste it in.
- **A featured image** (optional but recommended). See the image tips below.

Note: the site's address will change when we launch on birdseygroup.com. The editing steps will stay exactly the same.

## 2. Create the Article

1. Go to the editor address above and sign in.
2. In the left sidebar, click **News Articles**. You'll see the articles already on the site.
3. Click **Create New** (top right).
4. Fill in the fields below, then click **Save**.

## 3. The Fields

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The article headline. This also creates the article's web address |
| **Date** | The publication date, typed as `2026-08-14` (year, month, day). The site displays it as "August 2026" |
| **Category** | The small label above the headline. Choose one of: INSIGHT, EXECUTION INSIGHT, EXECUTION BRIEF, RELATIONSHIP STANDARD INSIGHT |
| **Excerpt** | One or two sentences summarising the article. Used on the home page card, in search results, and when the link is shared on LinkedIn |
| **Article Body** | The full article text; this is the main thing you'll be writing |
| **Featured Image** | The wide image at the top of the article (see image tips below) |
| **Display Order** | A housekeeping number. It does not control where the article appears on the home page; see section 6 |

### About the Web Address

When you type the Title, the editor automatically suggests a file name from it, and that file name becomes the article's address. "Execution Is the Standard" becomes:

https://birdsey-group.vercel.app/insights/execution-is-the-standard

You can edit that suggested name before the first Save if you want something shorter. **After the article is published, avoid changing it**, because anyone who has shared or bookmarked the old address would land on a "page not found".

### Writing the Article Body

The Article Body box works like a simple Word document:

1. Click into the **Article Body** field.
2. Type or paste the article text. If you paste from Word or Google Docs, the paragraphs, bold, italics, and bullet lists come along with it.
3. Use the small toolbar above the field for **bold**, *italics*, headings, bulleted lists, and links.

A few conventions the existing articles follow, worth matching:

- If the piece is bylined, the first line is the author's name in *italics*, for example *By Sandford Birdsey*.
- Use headings to break up longer pieces. The site styles them automatically; you don't need to bold or resize anything yourself.
- Keep the formatting simple. The design does the heavy lifting.

### Featured Image Tips

- The image runs the **full width** of the article and is cropped from the centre, so keep the subject centred.
- Use a **landscape 16:9 image, at least 1600 x 900 pixels**. Bigger is fine; the site resizes automatically.
- To add one, click the **Featured Image** field, then **upload** an image from your computer.
- This same image is used when the article link is shared on LinkedIn or by email, so it's worth having one.

## 4. Save Your Work

1. Click the blue **Save** button at the top of the form.
2. That's it. Your change is recorded immediately and the public site updates itself within about five minutes.

If Save is grayed out, a required field is empty. Title, Date, Category, Excerpt, and Article Body are all required. Scroll up, look for a field outlined in red, fill it in, and Save again.

## 5. Check It on the Site

Go to the article's address, which is the site address plus `/insights/` plus the file name:

https://birdsey-group.vercel.app/insights/your-article-name

Allow up to five minutes after saving for changes to appear, and refresh the page.

## 6. Feature It on the Home Page (Important)

Saving an article publishes its own page, but it does **not** automatically add it to the "Insights & News" section on the home page. That list is chosen by hand so you control which three or four pieces visitors see first.

To add your new article:

1. In the left sidebar, click **Home Page**.
2. Scroll to the **Insights & News** section and find the **Articles** list.
3. Click the small blue **+** button to the right of the "Articles" heading. A new row appears at the bottom of the list.
4. Click that new row to open it, and under **News Article** pick your article from the dropdown. The date, category, title, excerpt, and image all come across automatically.
5. Click **Save**.

To change which article appears first, **drag the rows into the order you want**. Hover over a row and you'll see a grip handle at its far left; click and hold that handle, drag the row up or down, then Save. The order in that list is the order on the page.

If the section starts to feel crowded, remove the oldest row using the red **trash icon** at the far right of it. Removing it from the home page does not delete the article; its own page stays live.

## 7. Editing or Unpublishing an Article

- **To edit:** click **News Articles** in the sidebar, click the article, make your changes, and Save.
- **To start from an existing article:** in the News Articles list, open the **⋯** menu at the right of a row and choose **Duplicate**. Handy when a series shares the same structure.
- **To take an article off the home page** but keep it live: remove it from the Home Page Articles list (section 6).
- **To remove it from the site entirely:** open the **⋯** menu on its row and choose **Delete**. Do this sparingly; the address stops working for anyone who has shared it.

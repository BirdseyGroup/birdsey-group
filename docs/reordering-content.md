# Changing the Order of Things on the Birdsey Group Website

Almost everything that appears as a series on the site (the brand cards, the news articles, the navigation links, the team) has an order you control from the editor. There are two ways it works, depending on what you're reordering. This guide covers both.

- **The editor address:** https://birdsey-group.vercel.app/admin

## 1. Which Method Applies

| What you're reordering | Method |
|---|---|
| Affiliate companies on the home page | Drag the rows (section 2) |
| Insights & News articles on the home page | Drag the rows (section 2) |
| Navigation and footer links | Drag the rows (section 2) |
| Repeating blocks inside the About and Birdsey Standard pages | Drag the rows (section 2) |
| Team members within a group | Display Order number (section 3) |
| Team group tabs on the About page | Display Order number (section 3) |

## 2. Drag-to-Order Lists

Lists that live *inside* a page are ordered by dragging. The Affiliate Companies list on the home page is the common example.

1. In the left sidebar, click **Home Page**.
2. Scroll to the **Affiliate Companies** section. You'll see one row per company, in the order they appear on the site.
3. Hover over a row. A **grip handle appears at the far left of that row** (a small set of dots).
4. **Click and hold the handle**, drag the row up or down to where you want it, then let go.
5. Repeat until the order is right, then click **Save**.

The public site updates itself within about five minutes.

### The Other Two Buttons on a Row

- The small blue **+** button, to the right of the list's heading, adds a new row to the bottom.
- The red **trash icon**, at the far right of a row, removes that row from this list. For a list of references, like Affiliate Companies or Insights & News, this only removes the item from *this page*. The company or article itself is untouched and still lives in its own collection.

### A Common Point of Confusion

Clicking a row opens it and shows a **dropdown** naming which company or article that row points at. That dropdown changes *which item* the row is, not *where it sits*. Order is controlled only by dragging the rows, never from inside the row.

### Where the Home Page Order Shows Up

Reordering the Affiliate Companies list changes the order of the brand cards and the scrolling brand showcase together, since both are drawn from that one list. Reordering Insights & News changes which article reads as the lead story on the home page.

## 3. Display Order Numbers

Team members and team groups each live in their own collection rather than in a list on a page, so they're ordered with a number instead: the **Display Order** field on each record.

- **Lower numbers come first.** A person with Display Order 1 appears before a person with 10.
- Team members are ordered **within their team group**, so each group starts its own count. Two people in different groups can both be number 1.
- If two records share the same number, their order between themselves is not guaranteed. Give each one its own number.
- A blank Display Order sends the record to the end.

To change someone's position:

1. In the left sidebar, click **Team Members** (or **Team Groups** for the About page tabs).
2. Click the person you want to move.
3. Change **Display Order** and click **Save**.
4. Open the records around them and adjust their numbers too if needed, so no two match.

**A tip that saves work later:** number in tens rather than ones. If your team is 10, 20, 30, 40, then dropping someone new into second place is just giving them 15, with nothing else to edit. If they're numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, you have to renumber everyone below the insertion point.
